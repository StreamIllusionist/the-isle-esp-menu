## 🧩 About

**The Isle Companion** is a curated list of The Isle open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, creature tracker, map reader
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — diet catalog, growth notes, stamina reader
- **Research Notes** — survival layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Creature Tracker — highlight nearby creatures in solo play
- Map Reader — visualize terrain and points of interest
- Stamina Notes — track stamina and hunger values
- Growth Tracker — review growth progress for study

### Wallhack
- Dinosaur Catalog — browse species, skins, and stats
- Diet Notes — organize herbivore and carnivore diets
- Skin Manager — keep custom cosmetics organized

### Aimbot
- Solo Study Notes — segment study on any map
- Weather Control Notes — adjust environment settings for study
- Movement Notes — review movement patterns in the sandbox
